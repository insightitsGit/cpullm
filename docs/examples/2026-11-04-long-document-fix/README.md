## Remaining boundaries

No repeated padding or new unsupported facts are added to reach a word target. Atomic source records can slightly exceed a target. Composition is bounded predicate joining; it is not unrestricted creative writing. Mechanism/causal explanations require their own evidence. Optional research suggestions do not become requirements or unsolicited missing-section notices in the email.
