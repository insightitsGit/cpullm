# cpu-llm 1.15.0: prepared for owner upload

This release adds two hypothesis nodes and natural-language routing to them. It also includes the grounded
inference expansion made after 1.14.0. It is built and validated here; the owner publishes it.

## New: Frontier node

- `Frontier: <topic>.`, `ask(..., frontier=True)` or `"frontier": true` on `/v1/ask` returns 10 to 50 ranked
  hypotheses (hard cap 50) built from relations across one authorized snapshot. If fewer survive, it reports a
  shortage instead of padding the list.
- It streams every relation combination around the topic within round, candidate, edge, memory and time budgets
  (defaults 1,000 rounds of 256, 200,000 candidates, 60 s). A stopped run is flagged `incomplete`.
- Each candidate passes three checks in order:
  1. abductive: the relations combine into a coherent explanation;
  2. deductive: exact source spans, no contrary or negating source, no rule contradiction;
  3. pre-thinker: rejects malformed, circular, single-source and restated candidates.
- Each entry carries `source_support`, `deductive_relevance` and `novelty` (0–1), with relevance scaled by term
  specificity, plus citations and a falsification test. Ranking is by source support, then relevance, then
  novelty, with no more than three of the leading entries sharing one term.
- Relations come from a spaCy dependency parse when the optional `prompt_nlp` extra and an English model are
  installed (`en_core_web_lg`, then `en_core_web_sm`), with a regex fallback. `FRONTIER_PARSER` selects a model or
  forces `regex`. When the appliance has an encoder, links between terms that share a head noun pass a cosine
  check.
- The first five entries are checked with Deepen and marked with their verdict; contested entries are flagged
  with the contrary quote. `frontier={"deepen_top": n}` chooses how many are checked (0–10).

## New: Deepen node

- `Deepen: <hypothesis>.`, `ask(..., deepen=<Frontier hypothesis | text | True>)` or `"deepen"` on `/v1/ask`
  examines one hypothesis link by link. It returns:
  - supporting and contrary evidence for each link, with a status (`supported`, `contested`, `counter_only`,
    `weak`, `missing`) and an overall verdict;
  - source predictions that name at least two of the hypothesis's terms;
  - existing setups that already measure one of its terms;
  - confounders: other causes of the outcome stated in the sources;
  - a test plan (mediation, 2×2 factorial or common cause) with what to vary, measure, block and control, and
    when the hypothesis fails.

## New: natural-language routing

- `classifier.specialist_plan` reports `frontier` and `deepen`. Natural prompts route to them only when no
  existing specialist claims the prompt, so existing triggers keep their routes.
  - Frontier: "What unexplored connections exist around X?", "What don't we know about X?", "Explore the open
    questions of X".
  - Deepen: "Check this hypothesis: …", "Is it plausible that …?".
- After a Frontier answer in the same `session_id`, "Deepen idea 3" examines that entry.

## Shared contract

Every Frontier and Deepen result is an `UNVERIFIED_HYPOTHESIS` with `promotion: blocked`. Nothing is written to
the index. The standard invention note is appended. Strict evidence mode disables both routes. Both are pre-graph
routes recorded in `routing.post_stages` and registered in the flag contract, the prompt-flag map and the OpenAPI
schema.

## Also in this release (after 1.14.0)

- Grounded inference integrated into research, Nammade and CrossDomain, sharing one prepared deduction per
  request.
- Temporal, inductive and abductive analyses over explicitly selected JSON evidence records.
- Clarified source-only APIs and evidence contracts.

## Evaluation

Three knowledge bases built from openly licensed arXiv papers are included as test fixtures:
`tests/fixtures/ai_bottlenecks`, `dl_generalization` and `consciousness`. Reviews are in `docs/reviews/2026-11-21-*`.
The hypotheses are candidates to screen, not findings: on these corpora a minority are strong research leads.
